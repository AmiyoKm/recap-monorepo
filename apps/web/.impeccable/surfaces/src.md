# Recap web app

Scope: every route in apps/web (landing, sign-in, dashboard, settings, profile, connected). Mode: Operate for the signed-in app; Persuade for the landing page, inside the same world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Recap looks like it belongs next to Linear and Raycast. It refuses the cream paper, serif display and terracotta dot of the previous theme, and every editorial costume.

OWN-WORLD: cool near-white ground, white bordered surfaces, zinc text ramp, one indigo accent for primary action, focus, progress, the logo mark and "today". Green only for a healthy connection, red only for errors. Inter at 13 to 14 px in the app, semibold headings with tight tracking, tabular numerals. 8 px controls, 12 px panels, 1 px borders, almost no shadow.

STORY: a signed-in user sees at once which work accounts are connected, what is missing, and where the digest will show. A visitor on the landing page sees an example digest and understands the account model.

FIRST VIEWPORT: app: light grey sidebar with the logo, Dashboard and Settings, user at the bottom; main column with a small page title and date, the Today panel and accounts on the left two thirds, setup steps on the right. Landing: left-aligned headline, one sentence, Get started; under it a full-width framed example of the digest.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user over the roll; seed key f90801cb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
